# Code of conduct

Project participation must remain professional, evidence-based, and respectful. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disclosure of private data, or disruption of the project are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement should focus on reproducible evidence, assumptions, trade-offs, and uncertainty. Research claims must be open to falsification. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct threatens contributors, users, or the integrity of the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
